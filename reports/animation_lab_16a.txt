Fight Royale - Update 16A: Animation Lab

Open the Godot project and use the Animation Lab button in the main menu.
Select a fighter and Idle/Walk/Run; click joint markers or use the bone list.
Use X, Y, Z sliders for joint offsets, gait sliders for stride, knees, arms, hip bob.
Save Fighter persists settings to user://animation_lab_profiles.json and (if writable)
res://data/animation_lab_profiles.json. Battles use the character's saved profile.
This is a live locomotion offset editor, not an AnimationPlayer keyframe timeline.
It does not modify VRM skeletons, skin weights, weapons, combat, sound, or other modes.
Godot in-engine rendering/playability still requires your local test.
